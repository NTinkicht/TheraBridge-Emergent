## Proposed authorized development scope

1. Offline deterministic fictional-evidence suggestion of draft home-plan activities and goals; no model call or live child data.
2. Restrict suggestions to authenticated assigned therapists and active center/child/service; reuse existing server-scoped plan evidence, authorization and optimistic version checks.
3. Provide source/version provenance and explicit uncertainty for each candidate, including missing or conflicting evidence and prompt-injection-style fictional text.
4. Clinician chooses, edits, discards, or explicitly inserts a suggestion into an editable plan only. No silent save, no clinical validation, no auto-approval and no guardian distribution.
5. Exercise isolation, revocation, empty/conflicting evidence, draft recovery, concurrency and responsive-browser tests; pass all six CI jobs and independent exact-head review before merge.
