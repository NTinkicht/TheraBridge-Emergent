# TB-EM-005 + TB-EM-006 — Trials and Center Operations

Tracking: TB-EM-005 is GitHub #24; Center Operations follows it. Status: IMPLEMENTED (synthetic only).

## TB-EM-005 trials (no billing)

- Every center gets `trial_ends_at = created + 30 days`. The migration backfills existing centers from their creation date.
- Limits are 3 therapist seats and 1 active child. Seats count active THERAPIST memberships plus pending, unexpired staff invitations that include THERAPIST.
- Limits are enforced on staff invitation, on adding the THERAPIST role, on reactivating a therapist, on child registration and on child restore. Each check runs under a row lock on the center.
- After expiry, every change returns 403 with a clear message. Reading keeps working, and invitation acceptance is refused.
  - Allowed after expiry (they only reduce access): removing a guardian, ending an assignment.
- A platform admin extends the trial by 1–90 days with a reason (`POST /api/platform/centers/:id/trial/extend`). The extension is recorded in history and audit.
- UI: trial banner with seat and child meters on the center home; trial card with "Extend trial" on the platform center page.

## Integrity and safety

- Double-booking is refused for both therapist and child, checked under the center lock.
- Appointments last at most 8 hours, end after they start (CHECK), and cannot start in the past.
- Cancel and no-show require a reason (CHECK).
- One active guardian link per email per child, and one primary contact per child.
- Guardians are always connected by accepting an invitation.
- Therapists see only children assigned to them and their own appointments.
- Guardian responses never include emails or dates of birth.
- No clinical data, notes or plans exist. Clinical capabilities remain DENY for every role.
