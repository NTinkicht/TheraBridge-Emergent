# TheraBridge Illustration Standard v1.0 — DRAFT for owner/clinical/design approval

**Roadmap:** M5-B, M5-13 (#196). **Status:** NOT APPROVED. No clinical publication authorized.

## One official art family
Semi-flat editorial illustration, clean geometry, restrained detail, gentle shading, quiet backgrounds, humane faces, realistic child proportions and consistent hands/equipment. Warm, calm, never infantilizing. No embedded text, watermark, signature, real child likeness, copyrighted characters, stock-photo mixing, anime, photorealism or unrelated render families.

## Output contracts
- Master canvas **4:3** (recommended 1600×1200); keep the central action and all safety-critical props fully inside a centered **1:1 safe crop**.
- Generate thumbnail/preview derivatives only from the approved immutable master; store crop/focal point, checksum, format and pixel dimensions. Small-card clarity is mandatory.
- Do not use a specialty hue alone as identity. Pair label and approved icon with restrained approved accent tokens. Preserve brand action blue, family teal and reserved violet.
- Same character proportions, facial rendering, hand/anatomy treatment, therapeutic props, camera level, light direction and scene scale.
- No patient-identifiable source or protected third-party artwork may enter generation prompts without separate rights and privacy approval.

## Five visual families (without changing persisted service IDs)
1. **Speech and language:** interaction, expression, receptive/expressive language; consistent communication boards/cards.
2. **Occupational:** daily participation and manipulation; real-sized tools and accurate hand/object contact.
3. **ABA / behavioral:** understandable learning/support context; avoid stigmatizing or coercive imagery.
4. **Physiotherapy:** biomechanically plausible poses and safe support surfaces; clinician must check technique.
5. **Group:** clear multiple-person participation without conflating group therapy with one-to-one specialties.

Existing feeding and developmental service values remain intact; their visual-family mapping is presentation-only pending clinical taxonomy approval.

## Required structured image brief
Template ID and immutable version; service ID and visual family; goal/skill; audience; step; materials; prohibited technique; safety warning; scene; approved character/prop references; style version; aspect/focal point; locale/alt text; intended rights scope.

## Review rubric: each independently pass/fail
Clinical accuracy, equipment placement, anatomy, no unsafe technique, readable thumbnail, reference consistency, appropriate representation, hierarchy, 4:3 and 1:1 crop, alt text, source/provider/license evidence, checksum, signed human decision. **Any failure => REJECTED.** Generation never auto-publishes.

## Release dependencies and sign-off
The reference board, five specialty scenes and character/prop references must receive an actual owner/clinician/design review before the standard can be APPROVED. WU4-B registers private assets in DRAFT only; WU4-C never shows unapproved image bytes to guardians. G2/G2b clinical governance remains NO-GO.
