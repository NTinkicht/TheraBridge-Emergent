# TheraBridge — commercialization roadmap

Status: planning; not a production-release approval.

## Business model
- **Cloud Starter / Professional:** annual or monthly hosted subscriptions under SaaS terms.
- **Enterprise:** annual term license for defined dedicated or customer-managed installations under separate deployment terms.
- All tiers retain ownership of the proprietary core with the **verified** legal owner. No routine source-code handoff. Source escrow, if offered, requires a separately approved contract.
- **Repository visibility decision (2026-11-09):** preserve the public GitHub repository to maintain current GitHub Actions/CI access. Proprietary commercial licensing does not require GitHub privacy; add a reviewed notice only after confirming rights.
- Pricing figures from early planning are exploratory, not offers, quotes or authorized rates.

## Engineering guardrails
- This document introduces **no** licensing or billing runtime behavior.
- Preserve tenant isolation, clinical controls, consent and safeguarding gates.
- Billing or license expiry must not cause unsafe loss of clinical access; specify emergency, read-only and lawful data-return behavior before engineering.
- Keep synthetic demonstration and production releases sharply separated.
- Production or commercial clinical use remains NO-GO until independent sign-offs.

## Contract clause map
Ownership and use restrictions; order term/renewal; permitted sites and seats; data roles; location and subprocessors; security and incident notices; support and SLA; updates; continuity/backup/export; retention and deletion; indemnities and liability; dispute resolution and governing law; enterprise installation and escrow.
